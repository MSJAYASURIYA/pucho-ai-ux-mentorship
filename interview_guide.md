# User interview guide

## Goal
Learn how people handle repetitive tasks today, where they struggle, what they need to verify before trusting AI, and how they expect to recover from mistakes.

## Participant note
The only response currently recorded in the project conversation came from a proxy participant rather than the target business-operations user. The participant named **studying** as a recurring task. This is recorded accurately but does not validate the assumptions about operations managers.

## Five open-ended questions

1. What task do you repeat regularly in your work or studies?
2. How do you complete that task, from beginning to end?
3. What part takes the most time or causes frustration?
4. What would you check before trusting AI to do it?
5. If AI made a mistake, how would you want to correct it?
